# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION / SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### Abstract
The patent landscape for Low Acrylonitrile NBR (Nitrile Butadiene Rubber) synthesis reveals a focus on emulsion polymerization processes. The majority of patents disclose the use of specific emulsifiers, initiators, and chain transfer agents to control the polymerization reaction. The acrylonitrile content in the resulting polymer is typically in the range of 18-46 wt%. Recurring chemistry includes the use of rosin acid or soap as emulsifiers, di-isopropylbenzene hydroperoxide as initiators, and tert-dodecyl mercaptan as chain transfer agents. Trends in the patent landscape indicate a focus on optimizing reaction conditions, such as temperature and reaction time, to achieve high conversion rates and desirable polymer properties. However, some parameters, such as monomer ratio and coagulation conditions, are not consistently disclosed across patents.
