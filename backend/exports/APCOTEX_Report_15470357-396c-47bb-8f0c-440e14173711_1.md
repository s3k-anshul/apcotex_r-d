# PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This technical report synthesizes polymerization and synthesis methodologies for Low Acrylonitrile Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (Low ACN NBR) based on a selected patent landscape comprising EP4112676, US9650502, US9034992, US7923518, US8389623, US8436091, and US8664340. The research scope focuses on emulsion polymerization techniques designed to produce NBR with bound acrylonitrile (ACN) contents typically ranging from 27.9% to 36.6%. The predominant synthesis trend involves cold emulsion polymerization conducted at temperatures between 7&deg;C and 8&deg;C to minimize polymer branching and optimize elastomer properties. Key process configurations include the use of advanced redox initiator systems (such as p-menthane hydroperoxide or diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide coupled with iron(II) salts and reducing agents like Rongalit C or sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate) and precise molecular weight control via incremental dosing of tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (t-DDM/TDM) chain-transfer agents. Furthermore, the patents highlight the critical role of post-polymerization coagulation conditions&mdash;specifically the selection of salts (NaCl, CaCl2, MgSO4, MgCl2) and pH adjustments&mdash;in determining the residual ion indices of the raw rubber, which directly govern vulcanization kinetics, Mooney scorch safety, and mechanical performance of the final vulcanizates.

#### Patent 2

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US9650502
- Patent Title: Process for the preparation of nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2017
- Polymer Type: Low Acrylonitrile NBR
- Relevance to target: Discloses detailed recipes for low ACN NBR (27.9% - 28.1% bound ACN) using incremental chain-transfer agent dosing.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Cold emulsion polymerization
- Monomer system: Acrylonitrile and butadiene
- Monomer ratio: 500 g acrylonitrile to 4.37 kg butadiene (Latex A) or 815 g acrylonitrile to 4.0 kg butadiene (Latex B)
- Water amount: 9.92 kg deionized water
- Emulsifier: Potassium stearate and DNMK
- Emulsifier loading: 0.53 kg of 5.4% wt potassium stearate solution and 0.440 kg of DNMK
- Initiator: Diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide (DIHP)
- Initiator loading: 6 g
- Catalyst / activator: Sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate (FSS) (139 g aqueous solution)
- Chain-transfer agent: Tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (TDM)
- Chain-transfer dosage: 18.72 g initial, with boosters of 2.34 g at 37% and 59% conversion (or 21.85 g initial and 5.45 g booster)
- Polymerization temperature: 7&deg;C
- Pressure: Vacuum applied prior to monomer charging
- pH: 8.5 - 8.6 (latex pH before coagulation); coagulation pH adjusted to 11.5 - 11.8
- Reaction time: 7 hours
- Conversion: 70% conversion
- Coagulation conditions: Magnesium sulfate (4 kg saturated aqueous solution in 43 kg water) at 45&deg;C, pH adjusted to 11.5-11.8 with KOH (14 g)
- Post-treatment: Filtered and dried in an air oven at 100&deg;C for 24 hours (residual humidity < 1%)
- Raw polymer properties: Bound ACN: 27.9% - 28.1% (Latex A/C), 33.9% (Latex B); Mooney Viscosity: 29.0 - 60.5 MU; Solid content: 15.2% - 16.2%

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Latex A was prepared by charging 9.92 kg deionized water, potassium stearate, DNMK, 500 g acrylonitrile, 18.72 g TDM, and 4.37 kg butadiene, initiated at 7&deg;C with 6 g DIHP and 139 g FSS solution.
- Incremental additions of TDM (2.34 g boosters) were performed at 37% and 59% conversion to control molecular weight distribution.
- Polymerization was short-stopped at 70% conversion after 7 hours using 515 g of 2% hydroxylamine sulfate solution.
- Latex was stabilized with 1% BHT antioxidant dispersion and coagulated using magnesium sulfate at 45&deg;C under alkaline pH (11.5-11.8) adjusted with KOH.

**Technical Relevance**
Demonstrates a precise cold emulsion polymerization process to achieve low bound ACN content (27.9%) with controlled Mooney viscosity using split TDM additions.

#### Patent 6

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US8436091
- Patent Title: Nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2013
- Polymer Type: Nitrile Rubber (NBR) with optional termonomers
- Relevance to target: Discloses emulsion polymerization using a specific iron(II) chloride redox system to produce NBR with bound ACN around 35-36%.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Emulsion polymerization
- Monomer system: Butadiene, acrylonitrile, and 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate
- Monomer ratio: 61.4 parts butadiene, 36 parts acrylonitrile, 2.6 parts 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate
- Water amount: 220 parts by weight (total water) or 2.1 kg water for 1.25 kg monomer autoclave batches
- Emulsifier: Texapon K-12 (sodium lauryl sulphate)
- Emulsifier loading: 3.0 parts by weight (general range 0.2 to 15 parts)
- Initiator: Trigonox NT 50 (p-menthane hydroperoxide)
- Initiator loading: 0.02 parts by weight (oxidizing agent range 0.001 to 1 part)
- Catalyst / activator: Premix containing FeCl2*4H2O (0.845 g) or FeSO4*7H2O (0.986 g) and Rongalit C (2.0 g) in 400 g water, with EDTA
- Chain-transfer agent: tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (t-DDM)
- Chain-transfer dosage: 0.54 parts by weight
- Polymerization temperature: 8.0&deg;C &plus;mn; 0.5&deg;C
- Pressure: 50 to 150 bar (during optional hydrogenation)
- pH: 7.0 &plus;mn; 1.0
- Reaction time: 6.5 - 7.0 hours
- Conversion: 84.2% - 86.1% conversion
- Coagulation conditions: Admixed with 50% dispersion of Vulkanox BKF (0.3% wt based on NBR solids) before coagulation
- Post-treatment: Washing with water (1 to 5 parts by weight per 100 parts NBR); optional hydrogenation at 50-150 bar for 2-10 h using 1-5 parts cocatalyst
- Raw polymer properties: ACN content: 35.3% - 36.6%; Mooney viscosity: 25 - 142 MU; Glass transition temperature (Tg): -23.1&deg;C to -25.8&deg;C

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Autoclave batches were run with 1.25 kg monomer mixture and 2.1 kg water using EDTA equimolar to Fe(II).
- Nitrile rubbers G and H were polymerized at 8&deg;C using Texapon K-12 emulsifier, t-DDM, and Trigonox NT 50 initiated by FeSO4 (Rubber G) or FeCl2 (Rubber H) premixes.
- The use of FeCl2 instead of FeSO4 in the redox system resulted in distinct polymer properties, achieving 84.2% conversion in 6.5 hours.

**Technical Relevance**
Demonstrates that utilizing a specific iron(II) chloride-based redox system instead of iron(II) sulfate yields NBR with modified Mooney viscosity and polymerization kinetics.
