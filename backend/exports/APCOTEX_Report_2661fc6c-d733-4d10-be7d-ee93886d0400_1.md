# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION/SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### 1. ABSTRACT
This report provides a comprehensive overview of the patent landscape related to Low Acrylonitrile NBR (nitrile butadiene rubber) polymerization and synthesis. The research scope focuses on the definition and production of Low Acrylonitrile NBR, with a selected patent landscape that includes five relevant patents. The major polymerization approaches include emulsion polymerization, with varying monomer ratios, emulsifier systems, initiators, and chain-transfer agents. The major formulation/process trends include the use of different acrylonitrile contents, butadiene/ACN ratios, and polymerization temperatures. The report aims to synthesize the extracted data into a cohesive technical report, highlighting the key findings and trends in the production of Low Acrylonitrile NBR.
