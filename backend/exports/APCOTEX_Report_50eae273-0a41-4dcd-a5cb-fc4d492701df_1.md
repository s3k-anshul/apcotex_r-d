# LOW ACRYLONITRILE NBR PATENT LANDSCAPE REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report evaluates the patent evidence provided for low acrylonitrile nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR) synthesis and related elastomeric material disclosures. The target definition is a butadiene-acrylonitrile copolymer with reduced acrylonitrile content, typically below about 20 wt% ACN, while retaining elastomeric performance. In the supplied manifest, only one primary patent was identified: US10443339B2, which is not a composition or polymerization patent for NBR itself, but rather a downhole elastomeric materials patent focused on carbon-based nanoplatelet-reinforced elastomeric components for high-temperature/high-pressure oilfield service. The patent explicitly references NBR among several candidate elastomers used in packer and seal applications, but it does not disclose NBR polymerization, monomer feed ratios, acrylonitrile content ranges, or synthesis conditions for low-ACN NBR.

Accordingly, the patent landscape in this dataset does not provide direct synthetic guidance for low-acrylonitrile NBR manufacture. Instead, it highlights the application context where NBR may be used as a base elastomer in harsh environments and where reinforcement with carbon-based nanoplatelets is proposed to improve performance. The disclosed formulation trend is toward elastomer matrix reinforcement rather than copolymer composition tuning. The process trend is component fabrication by mixing elastomer(s) with nanoscale reinforcement, followed by curing/cross-linking and installation in bore tools or packer assemblies.

From a low-ACN NBR synthesis perspective, the evidence is therefore indirect: it confirms the relevance of NBR in demanding sealing applications, but it does not support specific polymerization recommendations. No emulsion or solution polymerization parameters, initiator systems, emulsifiers, chain-transfer agents, temperatures, pressures, or conversion targets are disclosed for NBR preparation in the provided patent text.

## 4. CONCLUSION
The provided evidence does not contain a direct low-acrylonitrile NBR synthesis patent. US10443339B2 is best interpreted as a downstream application patent for reinforced elastomeric components, where NBR is mentioned as one possible elastomer but no NBR polymerization or composition-control data are disclosed. For low-ACN NBR synthesis, the evidence base here is insufficient to recommend feed ratios or polymerization conditions; however, it supports the need for formulations that preserve elastomeric integrity in HPHT and chemically aggressive environments. Based on the available evidence, no synthesis parameters can be recommended beyond the general requirement that any low-ACN NBR intended for such service should be compatible with reinforcement and curing strategies used in downhole elastomer components.
