# HIGH ACRYLONITRILE NBR PATENT LANDSCAPE REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report was requested for High Acrylonitrile NBR, defined as nitrile butadiene rubber with elevated acrylonitrile (ACN) content, typically above standard NBR grades and often targeted for improved polarity and oil resistance. However, the provided patent manifest contains only one primary patent, US11813892B2, and its disclosure is not directed to NBR synthesis, acrylonitrile/butadiene copolymerization, or high-ACN composition control. Instead, the patent concerns acrylated/acylated or acetalized polyol monomers and their homopolymers, copolymers, block copolymers, and statistical copolymers prepared mainly by controlled radical polymerization, including RAFT, for applications such as asphalt modifiers, adhesives, tire compositions, and fracking fluids. The examples focus on glycerol-derived monomer preparation, esterification/acetalization chemistry, and free-radical polymerization of those monomers with initiators such as AIBN and chain-transfer agents such as CYCART in toluene at 80°C. No acrylonitrile, butadiene, NBR, emulsion polymerization, solution polymerization of nitrile rubber, or monomer-feed-ratio control relevant to high-ACN NBR is disclosed in the evidence. As a result, the patent is only indirectly relevant through its mention of tire compositions and polymerization process control concepts, but it does not provide actionable synthesis guidance for high acrylonitrile NBR. The evidence base therefore does not support a true high-ACN NBR patent comparison; the report captures the disclosed polymerization chemistry faithfully and flags the mismatch between the target compound and the available patent content.
