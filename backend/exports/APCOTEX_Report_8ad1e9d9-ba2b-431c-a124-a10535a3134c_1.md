# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION/SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### 1. ABSTRACT
This report provides a technical summary of the research scope, definition, and patent landscape of Low Acrylonitrile NBR (Nitrile Butadiene Rubber). Low Acrylonitrile NBR is a type of synthetic rubber with a lower acrylonitrile content than traditional NBR, offering improved properties such as lower fuel permeability and better thermal stability. The selected patent landscape includes four patents from the European and United States jurisdictions, published between 2014 and 2019. The major polymerization approaches include emulsion polymerization, with various monomer systems, emulsifiers, initiators, and chain-transfer agents. The major formulation/process trends include the use of different emulsifier systems, initiators, and chain-transfer agents to control the polymerization process and improve the properties of the resulting rubber.

### 3. CROSS-PATENT COMPARISON & SYNTHESIS TRENDS
The selected patents show varying levels of detail regarding the polymerization process, monomer system, and reaction conditions. 
* The acrylonitrile content is only disclosed in EP3239198, with a value of 10.
* The butadiene content is not disclosed in any of the patents.
* The exact monomer ratio is not disclosed in any of the patents.
* The polymerization temperature is disclosed in EP2492306 (128) and EP3239198 (20), and US10208151 (1).
* The conversion is disclosed in US10208151 (97) and EP3239198 (45).
* The emulsifier is disclosed in EP3239198 (45).
The patents show a trend towards the use of diene-based rubber latex and acrylonitrile-butadiene monomer system. However, the lack of detailed information on the polymerization process and reaction conditions makes it difficult to identify recurring approaches or important differences.
