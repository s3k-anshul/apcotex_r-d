# LOW ACRYLONITRILE NBR

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report provides an overview of the research scope, definition, and selected patent landscape for Low Acrylonitrile NBR (Nitrile Butadiene Rubber). The target compound is defined as a nitrile rubber with low acrylonitrile content. The selected patent landscape includes patents related to the production of nitrile rubbers with varying acrylonitrile contents. The major polymerization approaches include emulsion polymerization and solution polymerization. The major formulation/process trends include the use of different monomer ratios, emulsifiers, initiators, and chain-transfer agents. The report aims to synthesize the extracted data from multiple patents to provide a comprehensive understanding of the production of Low Acrylonitrile NBR.
