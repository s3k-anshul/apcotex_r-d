# PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This technical report synthesizes patent research data on the polymerization and synthesis of Low Acrylonitrile Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (Low ACN NBR), focusing on a selected patent landscape comprising US9650502, US9034992, US7923518, US8389623, US8436091, and US8664340. Low ACN NBR, typically defined by a bound acrylonitrile content ranging from 27% to 36%, is highly valued for its low-temperature flexibility, oil resistance, and elastomeric performance. The patent landscape reveals that emulsion polymerization is the dominant synthesis pathway, predominantly conducted under cold conditions (7 to 8 degrees Celsius) to optimize polymer structure and minimize branching. Key process trends include the utilization of advanced redox initiator systems, such as diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide (DIHP) or p-menthane hydroperoxide coupled with reducing agents like sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate (FSS) or Rongalit C, and iron-based catalysts. Precise molecular weight control is achieved through the incremental addition of chain-transfer agents like tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (TDM/t-DDM) during polymerization. Furthermore, post-polymerization coagulation and washing processes are heavily emphasized across the patents. The choice of coagulant (magnesium sulfate, magnesium chloride, calcium chloride, or sodium chloride) and the control of latex pH prior to coagulation are critical factors in determining the residual ion indices of the final rubber. These ionic concentrations directly influence vulcanization kinetics, Mooney scorch safety, and corrosion resistance in sealing applications. This report provides a comprehensive cross-patent comparison and methodology extraction to guide future formulation and process optimization.

## 2. METHODOLOGY
### POLYMERIZATION RECIPE EXTRACTIONS

#### Patent 1

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US9650502
- Patent Title: Process for the preparation of nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed in the analyzed patent evidence
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2017
- Polymer Type: Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR)
- Relevance to target: Discloses a cold emulsion polymerization process for low ACN nitrile rubbers with bound ACN contents of 27.9% to 28.1%.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Cold emulsion polymerization
- Monomer system: Acrylonitrile and butadiene
- Monomer ratio: 500 g acrylonitrile to 4.37 kg butadiene (Latex A/C) or 815 g acrylonitrile to 4.0 kg butadiene (Latex B)
- Water amount: 9.92 kg deionized water
- Emulsifier: Potassium stearate and DNMK (disproportionated rosin acid salt derivative)
- Emulsifier loading: 0.53 kg of 5.4 wt% potassium stearate solution and 0.440 kg DNMK
- Initiator: Diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide (DIHP)
- Initiator loading: 6 g
- Catalyst / activator: Sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate (FSS)
- Chain-transfer agent: Tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (TDM)
- Chain-transfer dosage: 18.72 g initial, with booster additions of 2.34 g at 37% and 59% conversion
- Polymerization temperature: 7 degrees Celsius
- Pressure: Vacuum applied initially
- pH: Latex pH of 8.5 to 8.6
- Reaction time: 7 hours
- Conversion: 70% conversion
- Coagulation conditions: Latex added to 43 kg water and 4 kg saturated magnesium sulfate solution at elevated temperature
- Post-treatment: Washing with deionized water at pH 11.5-11.8 (adjusted with KOH), filtering, and drying at 100 degrees Celsius for 24 hours
- Raw polymer properties: Bound ACN: 27.9% - 28.1% (Latex A/C) and 33.9% (Latex B); Mooney Viscosity: 29.0 - 60.5 MU

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Latex A was prepared using 500 g acrylonitrile and 4.37 kg butadiene, yielding a bound ACN content of 28.1% and a Mooney viscosity of 60.5 MU.
- Latex C was prepared under similar conditions to Latex A, yielding a bound ACN content of 27.9% and a Mooney viscosity of 29.0 MU.
- Latex B utilized 815 g acrylonitrile and 4.0 kg butadiene, resulting in a higher bound ACN content of 33.9% and a Mooney viscosity of 32.1 MU.
- Coagulation was performed using magnesium sulfate, and the resulting crumbs were washed at pH 11.5-11.8 to control residual emulsifier levels.

**Technical Relevance**
This patent provides a detailed recipe for synthesizing NBR with low bound ACN (27.9% - 28.1%) using a cold emulsion process and a staged chain-transfer agent addition strategy.

#### Patent 5

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US8436091
- Patent Title: Nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed in the analyzed patent evidence
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2013
- Polymer Type: Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR)
- Relevance to target: Discloses a highly detailed emulsion polymerization recipe for NBR with an ACN content of approximately 35-36% using a specific iron-based redox system.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Cold emulsion polymerization
- Monomer system: Butadiene, acrylonitrile, and 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate
- Monomer ratio: 61.4 parts butadiene, 36 parts acrylonitrile, 2.6 parts 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate
- Water amount: 220 parts by weight (total water)
- Emulsifier: Texapon K-12 (sodium lauryl sulfate)
- Emulsifier loading: 3.0 parts by weight
- Initiator: Trigonox NT 50 (p-menthane hydroperoxide)
- Initiator loading: 0.02 parts by weight
- Catalyst / activator: Premix FeSO4 or FeCl2 (0.030 parts by weight) with Rongalit C (2.0 g in premix) and EDTA
- Chain-transfer agent: tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (t-DDM)
- Chain-transfer dosage: 0.54 parts by weight
- Polymerization temperature: 8.0 +/- 0.5 degrees Celsius
- Pressure: 50 bar to 150 bar (general hydrogenation reference)
- pH: 7.0 +/- 1.0
- Reaction time: 6.5 to 7.0 hours
- Conversion: 84.2% to 86.1% conversion
- Coagulation conditions: Admixed with a 50% dispersion of Vulkanox BKF (0.3% by weight based on NBR solids) prior to coagulation
- Post-treatment: Washing with 1 to 5 parts by weight of water per 100 parts of NBR
- Raw polymer properties: ACN content: 35.3% - 36.6%; Mooney viscosity: 25 - 142 MU; Glass transition temperature (Tg): -25.8 to -23.1 degrees Celsius

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Nitrile rubber G (using FeSO4 premix) reached 86.1% conversion in 7.0 hours, yielding a polymer with a Tg of -23.9 degrees Celsius.
- Nitrile rubber H (using FeCl2 premix) reached 84.2% conversion in 6.5 hours, yielding a polymer with a Tg of -23.7 degrees Celsius.
- The use of FeCl2 instead of FeSO4 in the redox system resulted in distinct polymer properties, including differences in the Mooney viscosity and ACN distribution.

**Technical Relevance**
This patent provides a precise, comparative recipe demonstrating how minor changes in the redox catalyst system (FeSO4 vs FeCl2) affect the polymerization rate, conversion, and final low-temperature properties of NBR.
