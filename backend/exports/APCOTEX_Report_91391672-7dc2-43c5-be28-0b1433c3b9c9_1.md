# LOW ACRYLONITRILE NBR PATENT LANDSCAPE REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report evaluates the single patent in the provided manifest for relevance to low acrylonitrile nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR). The extracted evidence does not disclose an NBR synthesis patent; instead, US9267019B2 is directed to functionalized silica, wet polymer silica masterbatches, and rubber composites that may include styrene-butadiene rubber latex and, in broader formulation language, NBR as one possible rubber component. The patent’s technical contribution is centered on preparing silica treated with multiple silane coupling agents, then incorporating that filler into latex-based rubber slurries and coagulating the blend to form crumb rubber composites. The disclosed process is therefore formulation- and compounding-oriented rather than a primary polymerization route for NBR.

Within the extracted evidence, the patent mentions NBR only as a usable synthetic rubber copolymer of acrylonitrile (ACN) and butadiene, and notes that nitrile content affects oil resistance and flexibility. However, no explicit acrylonitrile content, monomer feed ratio, or NBR polymerization conditions are disclosed for the target material. The examples instead detail silica functionalization with silanes, preparation of aqueous silica slurries, blending with polymer rubber latex at 70 degrees Celsius, and coagulation with coagulant. Water is clearly present in multiple downstream steps, but as a slurry, latex, and coagulation medium rather than as the core NBR polymerization medium.

Because the manifest contains only one patent and it is not centered on NBR synthesis, the report’s primary finding is that no direct low-ACN NBR preparation method is captured in the extracted evidence. The patent is still relevant as a downstream compounding reference for rubber systems that may include NBR, but it does not provide a disclosed low-acrylonitrile NBR composition or synthesis route.
