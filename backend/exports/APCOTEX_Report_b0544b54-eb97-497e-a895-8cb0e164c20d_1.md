# LOW ACRYLONITRILE NBR: PATENT LANDSCAPE FOR COMPOSITION CONTROL, MOLECULAR-WEIGHT REDUCTION, AND DOWNSTREAM FORMULATION USE

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report synthesizes four primary patents relevant to low-acrylonitrile nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR) and closely related low-viscosity nitrile rubber technologies. The target is interpreted as NBR grades with reduced acrylonitrile content and/or reduced molecular weight that improve processability while retaining the oil-, heat-, and chemical-resistance profile of nitrile elastomers. The patent landscape spans two distinct technology families: (1) direct or indirect modification of nitrile rubber by metathesis and hydrogenation to lower molecular weight and narrow dispersity, and (2) downstream compounding of low-viscosity or hydrogenated nitrile rubber into swellable or degradable elastomeric articles. EP2473279B1 and EP2057205B1 are the most synthesis-relevant. EP2473279B1 discloses metathesis of nitrile rubber in the presence of a transition-metal catalyst, followed by hydrogenation in monochlorobenzene using hydrogen pressure and a rhodium catalyst, yielding lower-Mw HNBR suitable for improved processability. EP2057205B1 discloses segment-interchange metathesis of unsaturated polymers, including HNBR, using Grubbs II catalyst or tungsten hexachloride/tri-n-butylmethyltin systems to form meta-block copolymers; while not a direct NBR synthesis route, it demonstrates how HNBR can be chemically reorganized into higher-value architectures. US10385647B2 and US10308807B2 are formulation/application patents rather than synthesis patents: the former uses nitrile elastomer/HNBR in swellable packers with powder superabsorbent polymer, while the latter uses NBR in dissolvable elastomeric composites with water-soluble binders. Across the set, the dominant process trends are emulsion-derived nitrile rubber feedstocks, metathesis-based molecular-weight reduction, selective hydrogenation, solvent-based processing, and compounding strategies that exploit low-viscosity nitrile rubber for injection molding, swellable packers, or degradable seals. The evidence supports low-Mw, low-viscosity nitrile rubber as the most practical route to improved low-acrylonitrile NBR performance, with composition control and post-polymerization modification being the key enabling levers.

## 3. CROSS-PATENT COMPARISON & SYNTHESIS TRENDS
- EP2473279B1 is the only patent in the set that directly modifies nitrile rubber chemistry to lower molecular weight via metathesis and hydrogenation; the others are primarily metathesis-based block-copolymer formation or downstream compounding/application patents.
- EP2473279B1 uses a solution process in monochlorobenzene with 1-hexene, a ruthenium metathesis catalyst, and hydrogenation at 85 bar H2 and 138°C; EP2057205B1 uses polymer-polymer metathesis in toluene or tetrachloroethane at 95-105°C with Grubbs II or tungsten hexachloride/organotin catalysts.
- EP2473279B1 reports low-Mw HNBR values such as Mn 19,000 g/mol and Mw 34,000 g/mol with PDI 1.78, whereas EP2057205B1 focuses on qualitative evidence of meta-block copolymer formation rather than target molecular-weight reduction.
- US10385647B2 and US10308807B2 both use nitrile elastomer/NBR in formulated articles, but US10385647B2 emphasizes swellable packers with powder SAP at 110-170 phr and 205 phr targets, while US10308807B2 emphasizes dissolvable elastomer composites with NBR at 50-70 vol.% and brine-triggered degradation.
- US10385647B2 includes HNBR and nitrile elastomer as functional components for high-pressure, high-temperature swellable packers; US10308807B2 includes NBR as a degradable particle phase in a water-soluble binder matrix for dissolvable seals.
- Across the set, solution-phase metathesis and catalyst selection are the main chemistry levers, while compounding patents focus on filler/SAP loading, brine exposure, temperature, and pressure resistance rather than monomer feed control or acrylonitrile content control.

## 4. CONCLUSION
The evidence indicates that the most technically relevant route to low-acrylonitrile NBR performance is not direct acrylonitrile-content adjustment in the extracted patents, but rather post-polymerization molecular-weight reduction and formulation engineering of nitrile rubber/HNBR. EP2473279B1 provides the strongest synthesis guidance: solution metathesis of nitrile rubber followed by hydrogenation can deliver low-Mw, low-PDI HNBR with improved processability, and the disclosed example set supports catalyst loading control and hydrogenation under 85 bar H2 at 138°C. EP2057205B1 shows that HNBR can also serve as a metathesis substrate for segment interchange, broadening the chemistry toolbox. For application-oriented low-viscosity nitrile rubber, the best-supported formulation window from the evidence is nitrile elastomer/HNBR combined with silica and powder SAP for swellable packers, or NBR combined with water-soluble binders for dissolvable elastomeric articles. Recommended evidence-based synthesis/process parameters are: solution metathesis in chlorobenzene-type solvent, ruthenium-based metathesis catalyst, controlled co-olefin addition where needed, followed by hydrogenation at elevated temperature and hydrogen pressure to obtain low-Mw nitrile rubber feedstocks suitable for downstream compounding.
