# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION/SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### 1. ABSTRACT
This report provides a technical summary of the research scope, definition, and patent landscape of Low Acrylonitrile NBR (Nitrile Butadiene Rubber). The target of this research is to synthesize and analyze the polymerization approaches, formulation, and process trends for Low Acrylonitrile NBR. The selected patent landscape includes US and EP patents, which provide valuable insights into the synthesis and production of Low Acrylonitrile NBR. The major polymerization approaches include emulsion polymerization, and the major formulation/process trends include the use of specific monomer ratios, emulsifiers, initiators, and chain-transfer agents. However, the extracted patent evidence lacks specific information on the acrylonitrile content and butadiene content, which are crucial for the production of Low Acrylonitrile NBR.
