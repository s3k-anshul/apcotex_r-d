# PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This technical report synthesizes polymerization and process parameters for Low Acrylonitrile Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (Low ACN NBR) based on a selected portfolio of six patents published between 2011 and 2017. Low ACN NBR (typically containing 27% to 36% bound acrylonitrile) is highly valued for its low-temperature flexibility and oil resistance. The analyzed patent landscape demonstrates that cold emulsion polymerization conducted at 7% to 8%C is the dominant industrial approach to synthesize these copolymers. Key process trends include the use of advanced redox initiator systems (such as diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide or p-menthane hydroperoxide coupled with iron(II) salts and reducing agents like sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate or Rongalit C) to achieve high conversion (70% to 90%) within 6 to 7 hours. Molecular weight and Mooney viscosity are tightly regulated using tertiary dodecyl mercaptan (t-DDM) or tert-dodecyl mercaptan (TDM), often employing incremental booster additions during the reaction. Furthermore, post-polymerization coagulation conditions—specifically the choice of salt (MgCl2, CaCl2, MgSO4, or NaCl) and precise pH adjustment prior to coagulation—are shown to be critical in controlling the polymer's ion index, which directly dictates vulcanization kinetics, Mooney scorch safety, and final mechanical properties.

## 2. METHODOLOGY
### POLYMERIZATION RECIPE EXTRACTIONS

#### Patent 1

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US9650502
- Patent Title: Process for the preparation of nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed in the analyzed patent evidence.
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2017
- Polymer Type: Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR)
- Relevance to target: Discloses a low-temperature emulsion polymerization process for NBR with low-to-medium bound ACN content (27.9% to 33.9%) utilizing incremental chain-transfer agent additions.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Cold emulsion polymerization.
- Monomer system: Acrylonitrile and butadiene.
- Monomer ratio: Latex A: 500 g acrylonitrile and 4.37 kg butadiene (~10.3% ACN in feed). Latex B: 815 g acrylonitrile and 4.0 kg butadiene (~16.9% ACN in feed).
- Water amount: 9.92 kg of deionized water.
- Emulsifier: Potassium stearate and potassium salt of disproportionated rosin acid (DNMK).
- Emulsifier loading: 0.53 kg of 5.4% wt aqueous potassium stearate solution and 0.440 kg of DNMK.
- Initiator: Diisopropylbenzene hydroperoxide (DIHP).
- Initiator loading: 6 g.
- Catalyst / activator: Sodium formaldehyde sulfoxylate (FSS) (139 g of aqueous solution).
- Chain-transfer agent: Tert-dodecyl mercaptan (TDM).
- Chain-transfer dosage: 18.72 g initially (for Latex A), with subsequent booster additions of 2.34 g at 37% and 59% conversion.
- Polymerization temperature: 7%C.
- Pressure: Not disclosed in the analyzed patent evidence.
- pH: 8.5 to 8.6 (latex pH before coagulation); coagulation pH adjusted to 11.5-11.8.
- Reaction time: 7 hours.
- Conversion: 70% conversion.
- Coagulation conditions: Latex (10 kg) added over 10 minutes to a mixture of 43 kg water and 4 kg saturated magnesium sulfate solution at 45%C, with pH adjusted to 11.5-11.8 using potassium hydroxide (14 g).
- Post-treatment: Crumbs (1.5 kg) filtered, washed with deionized water, and dried in an air oven at 100%C for 24 hours to a residual humidity of less than 1%.
- Raw polymer properties: Latex A: Bound ACN 28.1%, Mooney Viscosity 60.5 MU. Latex B: Bound ACN 33.9%, Mooney Viscosity 32.1 MU. Latex C: Bound ACN 27.9%, Mooney Viscosity 29.0 MU.

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Latex A was prepared by charging 9.92 kg deionized water, potassium stearate, and DNMK, followed by vacuum application and charging 500 g ACN, 18.72 g TDM, and 4.37 kg butadiene.
- Polymerization was initiated at 7%C by injecting 6 g DIHP and 139 g FSS solution.
- TDM boosters (2.34 g each) were added at 37% and 59% conversion to control molecular weight.
- Reaction was shortstopped at 70% conversion using 515 g of 2% wt hydroxylamine sulfate solution.
- Latex was stabilized with 1% wt BHT antioxidant dispersion before being coagulated with magnesium sulfate at pH 11.5-11.8.

**Technical Relevance**
Demonstrates that split-addition of TDM chain transfer agent during cold emulsion polymerization successfully controls Mooney viscosity in low-ACN nitrile rubbers.

#### Patent 5

**Patent Details**
- Patent Number: US8436091
- Patent Title: Nitrile rubbers
- Assignee: Not disclosed in the analyzed patent evidence.
- Jurisdiction: US
- Publication Year: 2013
- Polymer Type: Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR)
- Relevance to target: Discloses a highly detailed emulsion polymerization recipe for nitrile rubbers containing low-to-medium ACN (35-36%) using specific redox catalyst systems.

**Polymerization / Synthesis Method**
- Polymerization process: Cold emulsion polymerization.
- Monomer system: Butadiene, acrylonitrile, and 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate.
- Monomer ratio: 61.4 parts butadiene, 36 parts acrylonitrile, and 2.6 parts 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate.
- Water amount: 220 parts by weight per 100 parts monomer (total water in autoclave: 2.1 kg).
- Emulsifier: Sodium lauryl sulphate (Texapon K-12).
- Emulsifier loading: 3.0 parts by weight per 100 parts monomer.
- Initiator: P-menthane hydroperoxide (Trigonox NT 50) and Fe(II)SO4*7H2O or Fe(II)Cl2*4H2O.
- Initiator loading: 0.02 parts by weight Trigonox NT 50; 0.030 parts by weight Fe(II) salt premix.
- Catalyst / activator: Rongalit C (2.0 g) and EDTA in an equimolar amount relative to Fe(II).
- Chain-transfer agent: Tert-dodecyl mercaptan (t-DDM).
- Chain-transfer dosage: 0.54 parts by weight per 100 parts monomer.
- Polymerization temperature: 8.0 % 0.5%C.
- Pressure: 50 bar to 150 bar (for subsequent hydrogenation steps, if applicable).
- pH: 7.0 % 1.0.
- Reaction time: 6.5 to 7.0 hours.
- Conversion: 84.2% to 86.1%.
- Coagulation conditions: Latex admixed with a 50% dispersion of Vulkanox BKF (0.3% wt based on NBR solids) prior to coagulation.
- Post-treatment: Shortstopped with diethylhydroxylamine (0.2 parts), stabilized with Wingstay 29/Naugawhite antioxidant mixture, and coagulated.
- Raw polymer properties: ACN content: 35.3% - 36.6%. Mooney viscosity: 25 - 142 MU. Glass transition temperature (Tg): -23.1%C to -25.8%C.

**Relevant Experimental Evidence**
- Autoclave batches utilized 1.25 kg monomer mixture and 2.1 kg water.
- Premix solution contained 0.986 g Fe(II)SO4*7H2O or 0.845 g Fe(II)Cl2*4H2O and 2.0 g Rongalit C in 400 g water.
- Polymerization of Rubber G (FeSO4 premix) reached 86.1% conversion in 7.0 hours; Rubber H (FeCl2 premix) reached 84.2% conversion in 6.5 hours.
- Antioxidant stabilization was performed using a mixture of Sorbilene Mix (25 g), Naugawhite (38 g), Wingstay 29 (125 g), and water (63 g).

**Technical Relevance**
Provides a precise, reproducible cold emulsion polymerization recipe demonstrating that specific Fe(II) redox systems control branching and Mooney viscosity in low-to-medium ACN rubbers.
