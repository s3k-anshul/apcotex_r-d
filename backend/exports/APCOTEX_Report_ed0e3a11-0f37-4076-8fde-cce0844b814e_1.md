# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION / SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### Abstract
The patent landscape for Low Acrylonitrile NBR (nitrile butadiene rubber) synthesis reveals a focus on emulsion polymerization processes. The majority of patents disclose the use of acrylonitrile, butadiene, and optionally methacrylic acid as monomers. Initiator systems, such as redox systems, are employed to facilitate polymerization. Emulsifier selection and loading are critical factors, with anionic surfactants being commonly used. The monomer ratio, particularly the acrylonitrile content, is carefully controlled to achieve desired properties. Coagulation and post-treatment conditions, including the use of coagulants and adjustment of total solids content, are also important considerations. The resulting Low Acrylonitrile NBR polymers exhibit a range of properties, including solid content, Mooney viscosity, and tensile properties. Overall, the patents demonstrate a trend towards developing Low Acrylonitrile NBR with improved performance and versatility.

### Cross-Patent Comparison & Synthesis Trends
#### Emulsifier Selection and Loading
Anionic surfactants are commonly used as emulsifiers in Low Acrylonitrile NBR synthesis. However, the specific emulsifier selection and loading are not consistently disclosed across the patents.

#### Initiator and Chain Transfer Agent Strategies
Redox systems, comprising peroxo compounds, reducing agents, and iron(II)chloride, are employed as initiators. Chain transfer agents are not consistently disclosed.

#### Monomer Ratio and Reaction Control
The monomer ratio, particularly the acrylonitrile content, is critical in achieving desired properties. Acrylonitrile content is controlled between 17 and 45% by weight in some patents.

#### Coagulation and Post-Treatment Conditions
Coagulation conditions, including the use of coagulants and adjustment of total solids content, are important considerations. The coagulant dipping process is used in one patent, while others adjust the total solids content to specific ranges.
