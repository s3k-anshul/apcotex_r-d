# Low Acrylonitrile NBR
## POLYMERIZATION/SYNTHESIS PATENT RESEARCH REPORT

### 1. ABSTRACT
This report provides a concise technical summary of the research scope, definition of Low Acrylonitrile NBR, selected patent landscape, major polymerization approaches, and major formulation/process trends. Low Acrylonitrile NBR is a type of nitrile rubber with a low acrylonitrile content, which is typically below 30%. The selected patents provide information on the polymerization process, monomer system, and reaction conditions for producing Low Acrylonitrile NBR. The major polymerization approaches include emulsion polymerization and bulk polymerization. The major formulation/process trends include the use of different emulsifiers, initiators, and chain-transfer agents to control the polymerization process and produce nitrile rubbers with desired properties.
