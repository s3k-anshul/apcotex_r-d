# LOW ACRYLONITRILE NBR PATENT LANDSCAPE REPORT

## 1. ABSTRACT
This report evaluates the provided patent evidence against the target of low-acrylonitrile nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR). The patent manifest contains one primary patent, US10465031B2, which is not a synthesis patent for NBR but a downstream elastomer article patent focused on fluorinated nitrile rubber-based packers and seals for sour-oil and blowout-preventer service. The evidence explicitly discusses NBR, HNBR, and XNBR as elastomer families and notes that conventional NBR formulations vary acrylonitrile (ACN) and butadiene ratios to tune oil/fuel resistance and low-temperature properties, but it does not disclose a preparation route for low-ACN NBR itself.

Because the patent is application-focused rather than polymerization-focused, the landscape contribution is indirect: it confirms the industrial relevance of ACN content as a key composition variable in nitrile elastomers and provides comparative performance data for 17% ACN HNBR and 36% ACN HNBR in H2S permeability simulations. The disclosed examples center on fluorocarbon grafting of HNBR latex using Capstone MS-62 and tert-butyl hydroperoxide (TBHP) initiator, followed by drying and thermal grafting. These examples demonstrate that fluorination/grafting can reduce gas solubility, diffusion, and permeability, but they do not address low-ACN NBR synthesis.

Overall, the evidence supports the broader technical context that ACN level is a critical design parameter in nitrile elastomers, yet it does not provide direct low-acrylonitrile NBR copolymerization conditions, monomer feed ratios, or emulsion polymerization recipes. For low-ACN NBR synthesis, the most relevant actionable parameters remain those absent here: controlled butadiene/acrylonitrile feed ratio, semi-continuous or starved-feed emulsion polymerization, and explicit targeting of low bound acrylonitrile content.

## 4. CONCLUSION
The provided evidence does not disclose a direct low-acrylonitrile NBR synthesis patent. Instead, US10465031B2 is an application patent for fluorinated nitrile elastomer packers and seals, with indirect relevance through its discussion of ACN-dependent properties in nitrile elastomers. For low-ACN NBR synthesis, the evidence suggests the key missing process variables are controlled acrylonitrile/butadiene feed ratio, emulsion or semi-continuous copolymerization, and explicit targeting of bound acrylonitrile content below typical commercial levels. No validated synthesis recipe for low-ACN NBR can be extracted from this patent alone.
